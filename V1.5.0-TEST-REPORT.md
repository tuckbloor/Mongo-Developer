Therefore **the Windows `BUILD-INSTALLER.cmd` result is the authoritative compile/release gate**. Do not call the candidate released until it reports `ALL RELEASE-GATE TESTS PASSED`, `BUILD SUCCEEDED`, and the manual checklist passes against local MongoDB.
